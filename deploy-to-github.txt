Ready-to-use deployment steps for SIT SWIM Services

1. Go to https://github.com and create a free account.
2. Click New repository.
3. Name it: sit-swim-services
4. Choose Public.
5. Click Create repository.
6. Open the new repository and click Add files > Upload files.
7. Upload all files from this folder.
8. Click Commit changes.
9. Go to Settings > Pages.
10. Under Source, choose GitHub Actions.
11. Wait 1-3 minutes.
12. Your website will be available at:
https://your-username.github.io/sit-swim-services/

If you want to use your own domain later, you can connect it in Settings > Pages.
